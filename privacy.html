2. The short version

  • We do not use cookies.
  • There are no user accounts, no forms, no ads and no tracking across other websites.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your own device.
  • We count visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly statistics tool that does not use cookies and does not store your IP address. The statistics are private and only visible to us.
  • Our web host, GitHub, logs IP addresses for security reasons.

3. Hosting (GitHub Pages)

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

When you visit the website, your browser automatically sends technical data to GitHub's servers so that the pages can be delivered to you. This includes your IP address, the date and time of your visit, the page you requested, your browser and operating system, and the website you came from. According to GitHub, the IP address of every visitor is logged and stored for security purposes. We have no access to these logs.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is to deliver this website securely and reliably.

Transfer to the USA: GitHub participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Data transfers to GitHub are therefore based on the European Commission's adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR).

More information: GitHub General Privacy Statement

4. Visitor statistics (GoatCounter)

To find out how many people visit our website and which pages they look at, we use GoatCounter. GoatCounter is operated by Martin Tournoij, Ireland. The data is stored on servers of Hetzner Online GmbH in Germany and Finland.

The counting script is loaded from our own website. When a page loads, it sends the following information to GoatCounter: the address of the page, the website you came from, your browser and operating system, your screen size, your language, and your approximate location (country), which is derived from your IP address.

GoatCounter does not use cookies or any other storage in your browser, and it does not store your IP address or the full technical description of your browser. To count unique visits, GoatCounter links your IP address and browser to a random ID that is kept in memory for up to 8 hours and is not saved in its database. We only see aggregated statistics, such as the number of visits to a page, and cannot identify individual visitors.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is to understand how our website is used so that we can improve it and our games.

Your choice: You can object to this processing at any time (see section 8). You can also block GoatCounter with a content blocker in your browser.

More information: GoatCounter Privacy Policy

5. Saving your game (local storage)

Betula Grows saves your progress (for example, how Betula is growing and your gift collection) in your browser's local storage on your own device. This data is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Saving your progress is necessary to provide the game you have chosen to play (§ 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG). You can delete it at any time by clearing the website data for berkanagames.com in your browser settings. This will reset your game.

6. Contacting us by email

If you send us an email, we process your email address, your name (if you give it) and the content of your message in order to answer you.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is to answer your message. If your message is about a contract with us, the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

We delete your message when it is no longer needed, unless we are legally required to keep it.

Our email service is Gmail, provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Data may be transferred to Google LLC in the USA, which participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR).

7. Social media

Our home page contains links to our profiles on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube. The icons are stored on our own website. These are plain links: no data is sent to these platforms unless you click on them.

If you visit our profiles on these platforms, the platform's own privacy policy applies:

  • Instagram: Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, Ireland
  • TikTok: TikTok Technology Limited, Ireland
  • YouTube: Google Ireland Limited, Ireland

Some platforms provide us with aggregated statistics about visits to our profiles. Depending on the platform, we may be jointly responsible with the platform for this (Art. 26 GDPR). The platforms have full access to your data, so you can exercise your rights most effectively with them directly. You are also welcome to contact us.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • get information about your personal data that we process (Art. 15 GDPR);
  • have incorrect data corrected (Art. 16 GDPR);
  • have your data deleted (Art. 17 GDPR);
  • have the processing of your data restricted (Art. 18 GDPR);
  • receive your data in a common format (Art. 20 GDPR);
  • object at any time to processing based on our legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR).

To use these rights, simply send an email to playberkana@gmail.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. The authority responsible for us is the Bavarian Data Protection Authority: Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany, www.lda.bayern.de.

9. Children

Our games do not ask for any personal information, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this privacy policy when our website or games change, for example when we add new games or features. The current version is always available on this page.
